# TrustCom Risk Register
*Simulated case study — all people, data, and events are fictional.*

## Situation
TrustCom's risks lived in people's heads — nothing sized, owned, or tracked.

## What I did
- Identified and assessed 5 risks on a 3×3 likelihood×impact matrix, showing the work on every rating
- Assigned treatments (avoid / mitigate / transfer / accept) with named owners and due dates — no owner-less rows
- Recorded residual risk with sign-off on every treated row; rows close, never vanish

## Deliverable
Risk register — 5 risks, full columns (ID, description, cause, likelihood, impact, rating, treatment, owner, due date, status). Artifact goes in this folder.

## Skills demonstrated
Risk assessment · risk treatment selection · residual risk management · evidence-grade documentation

## Resume bullet
Built and maintained a risk register for a 25-person SaaS company: 5+ risks assessed via likelihood×impact matrix, treatments assigned with owners and due dates, residual risk documented and signed off.
